## Design Context

### Users
Single user — the developer. Used mid-workout on a Concept2 PM5 rowing ergometer, likely in a home gym or similar setting. Sweaty hands, elevated heart rate, needing information at a glance. Not a casual user; someone who tracks metrics deliberately and wants feedback without distraction.

### Brand Personality
Calm, focused, measured. The feeling of a precision instrument — a watch face, a depth gauge, a tide chart. Functional without being cold; disciplined without being aggressive. Not a sports broadcast, not a gaming HUD.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark — used during active workout sessions
- **Visual tone**: Instrument panel / nautical precision. Think: submarine readout, navigation chart, analog chronograph. Understated and purposeful.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid the "fitness app" aesthetic (neon gradients, motivational burst graphics, Tailwind dashboard card grids). Avoid the AI dark-mode tell: cyan accents, purple-to-blue gradients, glowing borders.
- **Colors**: Very dark blue-tinted background (not pure black, not GitHub dark). Warm amber/gold accent — creates contrast against the cool dark without screaming "tech." Muted, tinted neutrals throughout.
- **Typography**: Chakra Petch for large metric readouts (precise, tabular, instrument-like). Hanken Grotesk for labels and body text (restrained, legible at small sizes).

### Design Principles
1. **Legibility at a glance** — the most critical info (SPM, pace, watts, HR) must be instantly readable mid-exertion, at any distance from the screen.
2. **Calm hierarchy** — coach cues and secondary data fade into the background until needed; the eye goes to the numbers first.
3. **Instrument, not dashboard** — avoid the card-grid-with-identical-containers pattern; data should feel organized like a cockpit readout, not a SaaS product page.
4. **No decoration for its own sake** — every visual element either carries data or creates necessary separation. Nothing purely cosmetic.
5. **Quiet confidence** — the interface should feel like it knows what it's doing without announcing it.
